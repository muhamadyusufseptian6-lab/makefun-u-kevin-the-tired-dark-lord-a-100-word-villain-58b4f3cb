A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.
He conquered the Nine Valleys before breakfast — well, before lunch — and now
he signs grain requisitions for goblins who can't spell their own names.
The eclipse is coming, the Seventh Seal is about to open, and somewhere in
the middle of the ritual he forgets what the ritual needs. The tiredness
lives in the pauses, the stutters and the small deflections, never in a plain
announcement — and the sting lands in the last line.

Run it with Node 22 (stdlib only, deterministic):

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```

It writes the monologue to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (104 words,
558 characters, inside the 1–600 character gate).
